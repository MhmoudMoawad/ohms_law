**What is Ohm's law:**

    Ohm's Law states that the current 𝐼 flowing through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage 𝑉 across the two points and inversely proportional to the resistance 𝑅 of the conductor. The formula is:

***
$$
𝐼 = \frac{𝑉}{𝑅}
$$
​<br>
Where:

𝐼 : Current (in amperes, A)<br>
𝑉 : Voltage (in volts, V)<br>
𝑅 : Resistance (in ohms, Ω)
***

    This relationship is used to calculate the current, voltage, or resistance in electrical circuits.
