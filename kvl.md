**What is Kirchhoff's Voltage Law:**
***
    Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL) states that the sum of all electrical potential differences (voltages) around any closed loop in a circuit is zero. Mathematically:
***
$$

∑𝑉=0

$$
***
    This means that the total voltage supplied by sources in a loop equals the total voltage drop across all resistive elements and components in the same loop. KVL is based on the principle of energy conservation: energy supplied by the sources is entirely consumed by the components in the circuit.
***
